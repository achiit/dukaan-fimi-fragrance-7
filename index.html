About FIMI

A small shop built on one smell

AN

My uncle used to keep a small box of attars in his shirt pocket, and every Friday the whole mohalla knew he was coming before he arrived. That smell is why I started FIMI. I wanted people to wear something good without paying for a famous name on the box.

I started FIMI in 2024, selling from home to relatives and neighbours, and they sent their friends. I learned what suits our heat, our weddings and our Eid mornings, and I only stock what I would wear myself.

If you do not like something, tell me. I would rather lose one sale than a customer.

— Atif Nayyar, FIMI Fragrance
